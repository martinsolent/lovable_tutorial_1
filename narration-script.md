# Narration script – Prototype a mobile app with Lovable

Record each section as `audio/NN.mp3` (e.g. `audio/01.mp3`). The guide plays these automatically and uses the browser voice for any step without a recording.

## 01. Introduction: what is Lovable?

Start by watching the overview video. Lovable is an AI app builder. You describe what you want in plain English, and it builds a working website or app, with no coding. For user experience design, that means you can create a realistic prototype in minutes, test it on a real phone with real users, and update it from their feedback. And if you don't code, you can still build websites, apps and simple systems for your projects.

## 02. Go to lovable.dev

Open lovable dot dev in your browser. On the homepage, click the Get started button in the top-right corner.

## 03. Create your free account

Create your free account. You can continue with Google or Apple, or use an email address and password. The free plan needs no credit card and gives you five build credits a day, which reset at midnight UTC. You don't need GitHub for this guide.

## 04. Answer the welcome questions

Lovable asks a few welcome questions: light or dark style, your name, your role, and how big your company is. Pick whatever fits, for example Designer and Solo, and click Next each time. Your answers don't change what you can build.

## 05. Find the prompt box

You now land on the dashboard, which asks what's the vision. The box in the middle is where you describe your app. Check that it's set to Build, at the bottom right of the box.

## 06. Paste a clear prompt

Paste a clear prompt that describes your screens, navigation and look, and says it's for a phone-sized screen. You can copy our Closet prompt from the box on the page. A long prompt becomes an attachment. Click it to check the text, then press send.

## 07. Check the preview

After a minute or two, your app appears in the preview on the right, and the chat explains what was built. Switch the preview to Mobile to see it at phone size. Then improve it with small requests, one change per message. If something goes wrong, use History to go back.

## 08. Check your credits

Check your credits by clicking your workspace name at the top left. For more detail, open Workspace settings, then Plans and credit usage. It shows today's free build credits, any other balance, and how much each project has used.

## 09. Click Publish

When you're happy with your app, click the blue Publish button at the top right. Use the button rather than a chat message. Publishing from the button doesn't use any credits.

## 10. Check your website address

The panel shows your website address, ending in lovable dot app. Click the pencil to change the name if you like. Below it you can see who can view the site, and the result of a security check.

## 11. Choose who can view it

Click the visibility line to choose who can view your site. For user testing, pick Public, so anyone with the link can open it, then click Done.

## 12. Publish and visit your site

Click Publish. After a moment you'll see Your website is live. Click Visit site to open it. The live site is a snapshot, so later edits only appear after you publish again. There's no save button, because Lovable saves every change for you.

## 13. Send it to your phone with a QR code

On the live site in Chrome, use the QR code option in the address bar, and scan it with your phone's camera. Make sure it's the QR code for the published address, not the one from Lovable's preview.

## 14. Come back later from Projects

You can close your browser at any time. To carry on later, sign in at lovable dot dev, click Projects in the sidebar, and open your project. If the preview says Still building, click Keep building.

## 15. Open the published address on your phone

Open your published address in Chrome on your Android phone. If you have an iPhone, use Safari and follow the iPhone steps at the end of the guide.

## 16. Open the Chrome menu

Tap the three-dot menu at the top right of Chrome, then tap Install and create shortcut. On some phones it's called Add to Home screen, or Install app.

## 17. Install it as an app

Choose Install page as app, rather than Create shortcut, so it opens full screen like a real app. Check the name, then tap Install.

## 18. Find the icon on your home screen

Your app now has its own icon on your home screen. It may use the Lovable logo, which is fine for a prototype.

## 19. Launch it like a real app

Tap the icon. Your app opens full screen, without the browser address bar, so it looks and feels like a real app. Go through it yourself once, and then you're ready to test it with users.
